About Altitude

We started Altitude because good work kept getting stuck between departments.

Our mission is to give ambitious teams a single, accountable partner for design and engineering — so nothing gets lost in the handoff between them.

Our history

From a two-person studio to a full-service partner

2014

Founded in a shared workspace

Two freelance designers and one engineer took on a first client together rather than compete for the same contract work.

2017

First engineering hires

Design-only projects kept stalling at handoff, so we brought build capability in-house and stopped outsourcing development.

2019

Opened a second studio

Grew past twelve people and opened a second office to work across time zones with clients on both coasts.

2022

Added a growth discipline

Clients kept asking who would own the metrics after launch, so we built a dedicated growth & performance team to stay on.

2026

180+ projects shipped

Today we're a 40-person studio working with growth-stage and enterprise teams across fintech, retail, and healthcare.
